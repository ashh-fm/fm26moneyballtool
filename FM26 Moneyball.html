Squad

Shortlist

Add players with the ☆ next to their name in World Scout, or from their profile. The shortlist is kept between sessions. Players are stored by their game ID, so it carries over when you reload from the game.

Compare

Percentiles (0-99) are against every loaded player in the percentile pool. Players below the pool's minimum minutes show “–” and an empty chart.

Players

Every loaded player is listed. Scores are percentiles (0-99) against the percentile pool: players with at least the pool's minimum minutes (300 unless changed in Player search). Players below it are still placed within the pool but shown faded (a small sample) and sorted after pool players; players with no minutes or incomplete stats show “–” and sort to the bottom. Goalkeepers get stat percentiles but no role score. “Defensive actions” is approximated as tackles won + interceptions + blocks + clearances. Pts/£10k is the role score per £10k of weekly wage. Market value is FM’s estimate range, sorted by its lower end. Average rating, market value and age come straight from the game; positions list every position rated Accomplished (15+) or better. ☆ adds a player to your shortlist (My Club).

Compare players

Charts show percentiles (0-99) against every loaded player who meets the minutes limit set in Scout. For fouls, cards, offsides and goals conceded, lower is better, so the percentile is flipped.

All players

Raw columns are the values read from the game (or from your file). Calculated columns are per-90 stats and role scores (role scores only for players who meet the minutes limit set in Scout).
